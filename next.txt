## Design direction: "Honkish" (replaces the minimal/Apple-like direction)

Melo should feel like a toy that happens to be a great translator. Playful, tactile, and full of presence. Reference: https://benji.org/honkish. Keep the palette and logo, but change the personality: bolder, bouncier, more physical.

Principles
1. Presence over polish: you should feel your friend is in the room.
2. Every action gets a reaction: motion, haptics, and optionally sound.
3. Small delights, big restraint: no feature exists just to be busy, and every animation shows what changed.
4. Playful never blocks function: the original text and honest translation states stay one tap away.

Chat presence
- Avatar ring fills in over 400ms when the friend enters the chat, with a light haptic.
- When they leave, a short burst of waving emoji, or a "goodbye" in their language.
- Dashed avatar outline when the friend is online but in another part of the app (add a `screen` field to Presence).
- Typing bubble grows while the friend types, shrinks when they pause. Show the translated text only after send.
- Double-tap a bubble to drop a heart at the exact tap point (send relative x/y over Broadcast), with an expanding ring and a haptic on both phones. Store nothing.

Language-flavored delights (Melo-only)
- On friend arrival, a small "hello" in their reading language pops beside the avatar.
- The "Translated" caption does a small shimmer as the translation lands.
- Tapping to reveal the original uses a springy expand and a slight bubble tilt.

Little details
- Composer clear: a small trash icon fills as the message grows and shakes near the limit.
- Buttons squash slightly on press (scale 0.96, spring), release with a haptic.
- Copy-ID confetti of 6 to 8 tiny Ember dots.
- Empty state: an animated waving mark instead of text alone.

Type and shape
- System font, but use heavier weights and bigger sizes for message text (18 to 20) so the chat feels chunky and friendly.
- Rounder bubbles (radius 24), thicker avatars, more saturated Ember.
- Sound is optional: if added, use `expo-audio` with 3 sounds (send, arrive, friend joins), off by default with a toggle.

All of this ships in Phase 9, after the core chat works end to end. Presence and heart-tap use Realtime Broadcast only.
